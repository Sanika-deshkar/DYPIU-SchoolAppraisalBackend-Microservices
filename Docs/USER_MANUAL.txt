1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & QUICK ACCESS LINKS
================================================================================

The system consists of two web applications connected to a secure backend:

1. ADMIN FORM STUDIO (For Administrators):
   - URL: http://localhost:3005
   - Purpose: Manage universities, configure IQAC & VC accounts, customize
     form schemas, add/remove columns, preview forms live, and publish versions.

2. MAIN APPRAISAL PORTAL (For IQAC, VC, Faculty, Directors & Auditors):
   - URL: http://localhost:5173
   - Purpose: Fill appraisal data, upload proof documents, save drafts,
     manage faculty user accounts (by IQAC), review submissions, and download
     Excel/PDF reports.

Recommended Browsers: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari.

2. USER ROLES & ACCOUNT PROVISIONING ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

The system uses a clean two-tier user creation model:

Tier 1: Institutional Leadership (Admin Studio -> IQAC & VC)
- Created directly inside the Admin Form Studio UI without writing any SQL.
- When an Administrator onboards a university, they click "👥 Leadership" and
  create the IQAC Quality Cell Coordinator and Vice-Chancellor accounts.

Tier 2: Faculty & Auditors (Main Portal -> IQAC -> Directors & Auditors)
- Once the IQAC logs into the Main Portal, they go to the "Manage Users" page
  and create School Directors, Administrative Officers (Registrars), and
  Internal/External Auditors for their university.

3. PART 1: ADMIN FORM STUDIO (Form Designer & University Onboarding)
================================================================================

Access: Open your browser and go to http://localhost:3005

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. Onboarding and Managing Universities
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the top navigation bar, you will see the active University dropdown.
2. To onboard a new university:
   - Click "🏢 Universities" in the top navigation bar.
   - Click the "+ Onboard New University" button.
   - Fill in the university details:
     * Unique Code (slug): e.g. "mit_wpu" or "coep"
     * Full Name: e.g. "MIT World Peace University"
     * Institutional Domain: e.g. "mitwpu.edu.in"
     * Campus Address & Establishment Act
     * Primary Brand Color (e.g. #2563eb or #800000)
   - Click "Create & Initialize".
   - The university tenant is instantly created with isolated form schemas!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2. Provisioning University Leadership (IQAC & VC Accounts) - [No SQL Needed!]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Immediately after creating a university (or for any existing university), you
can set up its leadership accounts directly from the UI:

1. Method A (From Universities Tab):
   - On the University card, click the "👥 Leadership" button.
2. Method B (From Navigation Bar):
   - Select the university from the top dropdown.
   - Click the yellow "👥 Leadership (IQAC / VC)" button in the top bar.

3. Inside the Leadership Modal:
   - You will see status cards for "IQAC Quality Cell Coordinator" and "Vice-Chancellor".
   - Click "+ Create IQAC Account":
     * Full Name: Pre-filled or customized (e.g. "Dr. Quality Coordinator")
     * Email (Username): Pre-filled automatically with "iqac@{university.domain}"
     * Designation: e.g. "Coordinator, IQAC"
     * Password: Set a secure password (e.g. "Iqac@123")
     * Click "💾 Save Account".
   - Click "+ Create VC Account":
     * Pre-filled with "vc@{university.domain}" and default password "Vc@123".
     * Click "💾 Save Account".

4. Resetting Passwords or Editing:
   - If an IQAC coordinator or VC ever forgets their password, open this modal,
     click "✏️ Edit / Reset Password", type a new password, and click Save.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3. Viewing Schemas & Creating a New Draft Version
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click on the "📋 Schemas & Versions" tab in the top navigation bar.
2. You will see the appraisal forms available (e.g., "Academic Audit Schema" or
   "Administrative Audit Schema").
3. To make changes, you do NOT edit the live form directly. Instead:
   - Find the schema you want to edit.
   - Click the "+ Create New Draft" button.
   - The system creates a safe Draft Version (e.g., Version 2 - DRAFT).
   - Click "🛠️ Form Builder" to start designing!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4. Building Sections, Tables, and Input Fields
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the Form Builder Canvas, you can design forms with 3 simple building blocks:

A. SECTIONS (Major Parts of the Appraisal):
   - Click "+ Add Section".
   - Enter a Title (e.g., "Part A: Academic & Curriculum Activities").
   - Set Section Number (e.g., "A" or "1") and Owner Role.
   - Click "Save Section".

B. TABLES (Data Grids inside a Section):
   - Under your section, click "+ Add Table".
   - Enter Title (e.g., "Board of Studies Meetings Conducted").
   - Choose whether the table is "Repeatable" (allows users to add multiple rows)
     or "Fixed".
   - Click "Save Table".

C. FIELDS / COLUMNS (Inputs inside a Table or Section):
   - Under the table, click "+ Add Column / Field".
   - Enter the Label (e.g., "Date of Meeting").
   - Select the Field Type (see section 3.5 below).
   - Mark whether it is Required (mandatory).
   - Click "Save Field".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5. Supported Input Field Types
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can choose from 8 field types:
1. TEXT        - For short single-line text (e.g., Title, Name, City).
2. NUMBER      - For numeric values (e.g., Student Count, Intake, Marks).
3. DATE        - Displays a calendar date picker.
4. SELECT      - Dropdown choice (type options separated by commas, e.g.,
                 "Yes, No, In Progress").
5. TEXTAREA    - For long descriptions and multi-line remarks.
6. ATTACHMENT  - Secure document upload box for PDFs, Word files, or Images.
7. EMAIL       - Validated email address input.
8. URL         - Validated web link / website URL input.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6. Reordering Sections, Tables, and Columns
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Use the Up (↑) and Down (↓) arrow buttons next to any Section, Table, or
  Column to adjust its position.
- The new order is automatically saved.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.7. Testing with Live Form Preview
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the Form Builder Canvas, click the "👁️ Live Preview" button at the top.
2. The system renders your form exactly as faculty members will see it!
3. Test entering numbers, selecting dropdowns, and adding table rows.
4. Click "Back to Builder" when you are satisfied with your design.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.8. Publishing the Schema (Activating for Faculty)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. When your form design is ready, click the green "🚀 Publish Version" button.
2. A confirmation prompt will appear. Click "Confirm".
3. The system will:
   - Freeze the draft so it cannot be accidentally edited.
   - Mark this version as "PUBLISHED".
   - Make it immediately live for all faculty members on the Main Portal!
   - No code deployment or restart needed!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.9. Rolling Back to a Previous Version
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If you ever need to revert to an older form version, go to "Schemas & Versions".
2. In the Version History timeline, find the older published version.
3. Click "Rollback to this Version".
4. The system will instantly switch the active form back to that version.

4. PART 2: MAIN APPRAISAL PORTAL (Faculty, Directors & User Management)
================================================================================

Access: Open your browser and go to http://localhost:5173

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1. Logging In as IQAC or University Leadership
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open http://localhost:5173/login
2. Enter the IQAC email and password created in Admin Studio (e.g. iqac@dypiu.ac.in / Iqac@123).
3. Click "Sign In".
4. You will be directed to the IQAC Dashboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2. Provisioning School Directors, Officers, and Auditors (IQAC User Manager)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As the IQAC Quality Cell Coordinator, you can create and manage user accounts:
1. In the top navigation, click "Manage Users" (or "User Accounts").
2. Click "+ Add New User".
3. Select the User Category:
   - "Academic Director": Choose the School (e.g. School of Engineering, School of Design),
     enter Director Name, Email (e.g. director.soe@uni.edu.in), and Password.
   - "Administrative Officer": Select Post (e.g. Registrar, HR, Dean Placement),
     enter Officer Name, Email, and Password.
   - "Auditor": Choose Internal or External, select audit scope/schools, enter
     Auditor Name, Email, and Password.
4. Click "Create User". The user can now log in immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3. Logging In as Faculty / School Director
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Enter your Director Email and Password on the login screen.
   (Example: director@dypiu.ac.in / Director@123)
2. Click "Sign In".
3. You will be directed straight to your School Appraisal Form.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4. Navigating the Dynamic Appraisal Form
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The portal automatically loads the active published form created by your
  University Administrator.
- You will see tabs or navigation pills representing each Section (e.g., Part A,
  Part B, Part C).
- In the top header, you will see your School Name, Academic Year, and Current
  Form Status (e.g., "DRAFT").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5. Filling Form Fields & Adding Dynamic Table Rows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click on a Section to expand it.
2. Fill in the fields:
   - Text boxes: Type your information.
   - Date inputs: Click the calendar icon to pick a date.
   - Numbers: Enter numeric values.
   - Dropdowns: Select the applicable choice from the list.
3. Adding Table Rows:
   - For repeatable tables (e.g., Patents, Publications, Guest Lectures),
     click the "+ Add Row" button at the bottom of the table.
   - Fill in each column.
   - To remove a row, click the red "✕ Remove" button on that row.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6. Uploading PDF and Image Attachments
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Wherever an attachment is required (e.g., Meeting Minutes, Grant Letters,
   Certificates), you will see an "📎 Upload" button.
2. Click "Upload" and choose your file from your computer.
3. Supported file formats: PDF (.pdf), Word (.docx), Images (.jpg, .png).
4. Maximum file size: 25 MB per file.
5. Once uploaded, you will see a green "📄 View Doc" button. You can click it
   anytime to preview your uploaded file.
6. To replace a document, click the "✕" delete icon and upload the new file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7. Saving Drafts (Auto-Save and Manual Save)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. You do NOT have to complete the entire form in one sitting!
2. Click the "💾 Save Draft" button at the bottom right of the screen.
3. A success message "Draft saved successfully" will appear.
4. You can safely log out or close your browser.
5. When you log back in, all your entered data, table rows, and uploaded
   documents will be automatically restored.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.8. Final Submission
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Once all mandatory fields (marked with a red asterisk *) are completed:
2. Review your entries across all sections.
3. Click the "📤 Submit Appraisal" button.
4. A confirmation dialogue will display asking if you are ready to finalize.
5. Click "Confirm Submission".
6. Your form status will change to "SUBMITTED" and lock into read-only mode.
7. Your appraisal is now forwarded to the IQAC, Reviewers, and Auditors.

5. PART 3: REVIEW & EVALUATION (IQAC, Vice-Chancellor & Auditors)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1. Viewing Submitted School Appraisals
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log in as IQAC (iqac@dypiu.ac.in), Vice-Chancellor (vc@dypiu.ac.in), or
   Auditor (auditor.ac@dypiu.ac.in).
2. Go to the "Review Dashboard" or "Submissions Queue".
3. You will see a table listing all submitted school reports, showing:
   - School Name
   - Director / Submitter Name
   - Academic Year
   - Submission Date
   - Current Audit Status (e.g., "SUBMITTED", "UNDER_REVIEW", "APPROVED")
4. Click "Review Form" or "View Submission" on any row to open the complete report.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2. Verifying Uploaded Documents
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Inside the submission view, all uploaded proofs will appear with clickable
  "📄 View Doc" links.
- Click any document link to open and inspect the proof in a new tab.
- To download all attachments for that school at once, click the
  "📦 Download Attachments (.zip)" button.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3. Providing Review Remarks & Scores
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Scroll to the "Review & Evaluation Section" at the bottom.
2. Enter your Evaluator Remarks, Quality Recommendations, or SWOC Feedback.
3. If scoring is enabled, enter marks for each criterion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4. Approving, Forwarding, or Requesting Changes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose the appropriate action:
- "Forward to Auditor": Assigns the submission to internal/external audit panels.
- "Approve Appraisal": Marks the appraisal as officially approved by IQAC/VC.
- "Request Revisions / Send Back": Sends the form back to the Director with
  your comments so they can edit and re-submit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5. Exporting Reports to Excel and PDF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the top right of the review page, you have 3 export options:
1. "📊 Export to Excel (.xlsx)":
   - Downloads a spreadsheet with structured sheets for each form section.
2. "📄 Export to PDF (.pdf)":
   - Generates a print-ready, formatted institutional audit report with
     university header and branding.
3. "📚 Consolidated University Excel":
   - (For IQAC/VC) Downloads a single master workbook comparing all schools
     across the university side-by-side.

6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q1: Do I need to run SQL queries in pgAdmin when adding a new university?
A1: NO! You can do everything from the Admin Form Studio UI (http://localhost:3005).
    Create the university, click "👥 Leadership", and create the IQAC and VC accounts.

Q2: I made a change in the Admin Form Studio, but faculty do not see it. Why?
A2: Make sure you clicked the green "🚀 Publish Version" button in the Form
    Studio. Changes in "DRAFT" versions are not visible to users until published.

Q3: What file formats and sizes are allowed for proof attachments?
A3: PDF documents (.pdf), Word documents (.docx), and Images (.jpg, .png).
    The file size limit is 25 MB per attachment.

Q4: Can I edit my appraisal form after clicking "Submit"?
A4: Once submitted, the form is locked to prevent accidental modifications during
    the official audit. If you need to make changes, contact the IQAC reviewer
    to "Send Back" the submission for revision.

Q5: How do I switch between different Universities?
A5: In the Admin Form Studio, click the University selector dropdown in the top
    navigation bar to switch between institutions.

Q6: What happens if my internet disconnects while filling the form?
A6: You can click "Save Draft" frequently. If your session expires, simply sign
    in again and your saved draft data will be loaded automatically.
